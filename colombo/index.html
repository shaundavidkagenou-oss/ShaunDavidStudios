The World

The Island Capital, Open to Explore

A sprawling, freely explorable Sri Lankan city rendered with the studio's signature golden-hour atmosphere and trilingual street-level detail.

Colombo: Shadows of the Island places players inside a living reimagining of Colombo — neon-lit night markets, harbour docks, rain-slicked downtown streets, and quiet coastal roads — where every district carries its own rhythm, light, and secrets.

Move through the city at your own pace, uncover the stories moving through its shadows, and experience an original Sri Lankan saga built from the ground up by Shaun David Studios — the studio's most ambitious project to date.

  • Open-World Colombo
  • Original Sri Lankan Saga
  • Golden-Hour Atmosphere
  • Trilingual Street Detail
